The festival

Five days of light

Diwali is a five-day celebration. Here is a quick look at each day.

1

Dhanteras

Homes are cleaned and people buy something auspicious, often gold, silver or utensils.

2

Naraka Chaturdashi

Also called Choti Diwali. Diyas are lit to mark light winning over darkness.

3

Lakshmi Puja

The main day. Families light diyas and pray to Goddess Lakshmi for prosperity.

4

Govardhan Puja

Observed with offerings of food in many regions. In some places it marks the new year.

5

Bhai Dooj

Siblings meet and celebrate their bond with a tilak and sweets.

Traditions and the order of observances vary by region and community.
